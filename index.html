Challenges faced by Students

Pakistani students struggles alot when it comes to education .They not only face mental problems but also financial issues. Following are the main Challenges faced by them:

Financial Barriers:

  • Tuition and academy fees.
  • Expensive books and study materials.
  • Students having to compromise because of limited finances.
  • Transportation expenses.

Education System Issues:

  • Less opportunity to explore creativity and critical thinking.
  • Limited practical learning.
  • Too much focus on grades and memorization.
  • Outdated teaching methods.

Family and social Expectations:

  • Feeling that your own interests aren't always understood.
  • Expectations to always perform well.
  • Being compared with siblings, cousins, or classmates.
  • Less opportunity to explore creativity and critical thinking.

Academic Pressure:

  • Competitive admission processes.
  • Constant pressure to score higher.
  • Fear of failing or disappointing others.
  • Heavy syllabus and endless exams.
  • Feeling like one result determines your future.
